# Recommended Reading

A curated list of resources that shaped the tools and techniques in JusotLabs. All books listed here are available through official publishers — support the authors who made this knowledge possible.

- **The Web Application Hacker's Handbook** — Stuttard & Pinto (Wiley)
- **Web Hacking 101** — Peter Yaworski
- **Bug Bounty Bootcamp** — Vickie Li (No Starch Press)
